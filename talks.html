Why You Keep Rewatching the Same TV Show

TEDxAUA · 2024

Why do we return to the same television shows even when there is seemingly endless new entertainment available? This talk explores the appeal of familiarity, repetition, and the worlds we return to again and again.

This talk was presented at TEDxAUA in 2024. It received Editor's Choice at the American University of Armenia and was subsequently featured on the main TED YouTube channel.
