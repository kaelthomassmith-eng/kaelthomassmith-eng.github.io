Absurdist comedy2018 · Pamplemousse

Bill Bradney Has a Problem

Bill Bradney has a simple problem: he is stressed about the cost of seeing his psychiatrist. Unfortunately, the only way he can address that stress is by continuing to see his psychiatrist. What begins as a therapy session quickly becomes an increasingly absurd argument about money, anxiety, time, and the impossibility of solving a problem that depends upon its own existence.

Media satire2014 · Gravel

Primetime

A mental hospital becomes the setting for an absurd epidemic: patients believe they are characters from television, from Sherlock Holmes and Columbo to Walter White and Gilligan. As the boundary between fiction and reality begins to collapse, the doctors may not be as separate from the patients as they think.

Doctor’s Orders

When a wealthy businessman tells his doctor that his wife is poisoning him, the diagnosis seems obvious: paranoia. But as his health deteriorates and the doctor’s advice becomes increasingly consequential, the distinction between treatment and conspiracy begins to disappear.

The Cask of Amontillado Distilled

A comic sequel to Edgar Allan Poe’s “The Cask of Amontillado,” in which Montresor’s carefully planned revenge goes disastrously wrong. After walling Fortunato into the family catacombs, Montresor discovers that he has somehow trapped himself as well—and must now rely on the man he intended to kill.
