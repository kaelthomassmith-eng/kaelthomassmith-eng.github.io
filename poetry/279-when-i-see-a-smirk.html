When I See a Smirk

Published in Neologism, Issue #10, March 2018; Accepted by Ginosko Literary Journal, forthcoming in Issue 37
When I see a smirk flutter ‘cross your face I try to follow it with mil’tant gaze. Shall I see it upon your cheek in chase? Or will it be but a’ sly, imagined haze? Or perhaps your lips with a gentle crease Will tell me what you keep under lock n’ key - The fine lines of some kiss-in-waiting free Those lines from out of which you try to cease. Might your hand reveal with nervous jitter, Palsy out with a’ twitter, th’ secret you keep - While butterflies bred of numbrous litter Are caged behind your fat windows ev’r deep. So in a marathon of sight I sprint In mirth, to find some shy and living hint.
