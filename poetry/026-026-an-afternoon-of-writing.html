An Afternoon of “writing”

*Published in Pilcrow and Dagger in 2018
*Republished in either Feed the Holy or Masticadores USA in 2026

(had a renegade thought --

committed some kind of authorial sin.

Had given an opinion,

and negativity flew into the room.

Won’t put on any solids --

I wanted to type out a ballad

for all the airs we need to breathe,

but my fingers are all in a knot.

Got lost in my kitchen,

walking on pins of rue

but all I could do was

draw circles with my feet.

Wish I’d had the strength to get up

and take another shot;

aestive is my forehead

as I try and solve the world in a sec.

Now I’m staring at the ceiling

howling up at myself, ‘good luck,’

but I’m on the floor

telling myself I’m not stuck

Put on a smile and got away with it

Because ‘fore this white void

there was no one there

to call my bluff)

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
