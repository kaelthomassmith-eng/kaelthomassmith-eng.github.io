Three Oddest Words

Published in UnLost in 2019 / Republished in Ginosko in 2026 · Journal
I destroy words even as I speak them My lips disassembling them outright: Future – the first syllable already In the past by the time it manifests. Silence -- decimated by a’ wreckingball Of a consonants spoken over the teeth. Nothing – a poor word betraying itself On my lip’s a priori insistence.
