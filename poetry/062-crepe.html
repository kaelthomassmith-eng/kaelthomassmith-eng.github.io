Crepe

Published in ELLIE in 2026
In which the beauty of the world is folded in on itself like a beautiful crepe, shimmying in a hot pan, and soon laid to rest in my mouth -- a caul of chocolate covering it through and through: such is the morning of a lazy Sunday, when all is well, and well is all.
