Poem 87

Double Vision

*Published in Valley Voices in 2019
The department of forgetting is where I worked
The deception of forgiving is what I worked
 
Indeed, I was only a lost person
Included, I was any a lot person
 
When the stubble of history coarses our chins
When the stumble off history causes our wins
 
For this we can toast to tonight
For this we can talk to no night
 
Taking the bottle up to our lips
Taking the battle up to our kids
 
To quaff down the river Styx
To quit down the liver sick
 
When the flag’s underneath your seat
When the fog’s underneath, you see
 
To elevate your mind and glass
To enliven your kind and loss
 
I’ve been tested when to exit this bar
I’ve been tasted what’s to eat in this bar
 
But never asked to enter.
But never masked to enter.
 
Broken hearts flee the body’s oppression
Broken hearts flee somebody’s oppression.
