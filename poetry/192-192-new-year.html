New Year

*Published in Southern Poetry Review in 2026 / Republished in Virginia Writers Project, Summer 2026, p. 42

The new year sneaks up on us

Every year, yet before it clasps

Its hand over our eyes, we act

Like we’ve seen it coming –

announcing its presence

with loud shouts and popping sirens

to warn everyone

of the stalker,

Who drags us into the new year

Whether we like it or not.

New Year

It sneaks up on us

every time, yet before it clamps

its hand over our eyes, we act

like we've seen it coming---

announcing its presence

with loud shouts and popping corks,

even sirens, to warn everyone of the stalker

who will drag us away.
