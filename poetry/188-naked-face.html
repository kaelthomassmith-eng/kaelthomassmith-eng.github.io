Naked Face

Published in BareBackMag in 2026
Should the sky unfold, and reveal your Face, Which holds all the shyness of something more erotic -- The smoke from your cigarette a teasing chiffon -- Oh, then might the heavens themselves hide; Your nude eyes Have the nerve to foreshadow a torrent of tears while huddling with each other. Meanwhile, your lashes indecently flutter, with a movement more lustful than a bachata To keep court with some roue or another -- Whoever espies you across the way, Even if that be miles, To see that face, To be in likeness with the world itself -- Unclothed brows might be said to be erect, and in that thought you can read in my mind -- not to say you’re a coquette, but just that it’s on danger I’d bet.
