Ship of Fools

Published in EgoPHobia in 2016; Republished in The Fictional Café, 5 October 2020
This heavy Ship of Fools so soon cast off - The gentleman onboard, their hats they doff. Heads t’ follow, our thoughts dear but abaft are Stuffing that light in th’ madness of May ‘far. The court is a’ jester with an inf’nite smile. Hark! The two-headed coin of the gryllos Finds many maps in skel’tal trees virile – S’ they rest their many mouths on lead pillows. This sea of nothing, down our throats we quaff. O lo, we spiral down this dark Passion pit From Madrid’s silent tower falling off To jerk our movements into another fit. Do cold showers overhead skirt this place… For this Tree o’ Knowledge has a happy face?
