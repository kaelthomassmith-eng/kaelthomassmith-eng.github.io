Saturday Night

Published in Zombie Logic Review in 2013-2018
I was me, which was me for you. We watched [Censored] TV (It’s a movie adapted from an e.e. cummings poem) And ate caramelized fossils from a healthier age… ‘Cause every little thing is littler than a thought. Our popcorn smelled like a weird memory, And the couch hugged us tighter than we dared each other.
