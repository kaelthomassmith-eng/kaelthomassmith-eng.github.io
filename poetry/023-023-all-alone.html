Poem 23

All Alone

*published in Chewers by Masticadores in 2026
Published version ↗   Journal / archive ↗
My sight is through a wat’ry haze
Gasping in a’ all too teary gaze,
Whose self, water soon overtakes,
Submerging my head in shiv’red quakes.
Its tight surface my hand will pierce,
But only touch air, its grasp fierce,
And, as I sink, my heart in fear -
Would anybody even care?
He who’s not worth a heartfelt moan -
I am nothing, but all alone.
