City Lights

There are 574 total words in the intertitles of Chaplin’s magnum opus City Lights. This homage poem is constructed from those words alone.; Published in UnLost in 2018; Republished in Impspired somewhere around 2018? · Published/online version
Tomorrow, The people will face life, the sober dawn shall take possession, a different man has a fever and… tomorrow the birds shall sing To the people of this city: our prosperity, like flowers, the fever will burn up. For the cure, We will donate our prosperity: Rent your attention, rent your face, your home your car. Today, the people will face the autumn conquest. The people will face it sober: Split your home, swell your news, for nothing but what’s returned.
