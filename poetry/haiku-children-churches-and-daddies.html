#122 — Untitled (clear)

Children, Churches and Daddies (cc&d), v304, December 2020 ('Reptile Lust' issue; collected in Roll the Bones, Scars Publications).

#123 — Untitled (blush)

Children, Churches and Daddies (cc&d), v304, December 2020 ('Reptile Lust' issue; collected in Roll the Bones, Scars Publications).

The sun’s alarm beeps - Too late – and the girlish trees All blush a deep red.
