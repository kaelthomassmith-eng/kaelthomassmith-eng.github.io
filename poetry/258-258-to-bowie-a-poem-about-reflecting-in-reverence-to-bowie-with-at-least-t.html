Poem 258

To Bowie

*Published in Door is a Jar in 2019
...and your dreams are so close to your face
that they gave you a black eye.
(We sat down on the couch and kept falling,
looking for Major Tom – our drinks rye).
 
The glam stars hung loosely above us,
where man made a cliché of god’s beauty,
so turn the record on its spindle
but only if its rotation is a rebellious duty.
 
Sitting, we passed upon the stairs
to meet a pretty thing, who only
gingerbreaded up her eyes, sans gaze,
so that we existed together lonely.
 
We died with our mouths open
from the memory of a free will,
before the hologramic machine
took our ashes and made our fame kill.
 
Let’s soar my friend to the greatest heights
with a wink and a satanic grin.
How sad if the planets above didn’t dance
with beautiful and feigned indifference.
