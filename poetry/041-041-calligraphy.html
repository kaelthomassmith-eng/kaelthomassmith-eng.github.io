Calligraphy

*Published in Calliope in 2019

Where the word’s beauty could not be trusted on the page

it must be written out,

treste.

Our hands manufacture the word

following the mind

upon the kine of a truth,

whose delicate understanding

builds upon it in fine curves

and

draws the timeline of the word’s utterance:

a castanet of consonants and --

glides

unto its growth in the mind.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
