The Dreamer

Published in Wood Coin in 2017
What can a dreamer ever be If that dreamer do be me? For what to do when I see you Lying next to me? When we lie entwined and aligned, Then my mind can never find If what I gleam is but a dream Or of a waked mind Since in yourself I know perfection, With absolutely no exception, Then in this late nocturnal fete Dreams and reality do conflate.
