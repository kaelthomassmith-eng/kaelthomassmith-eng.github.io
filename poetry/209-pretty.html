“Pretty”

Published in Virginia Writers Project, Summer 2026, p. 43
I do want to be called pretty before anything else – A butterfly phrase to my inner heart: for it is only humanity’s stuck up conceit (self-congratulating) that makes them convince themselves that they care about anything more than this.
