Kintsugi

Kintsugi (金継ぎ,) : broken pottery is visibly repaired using lacquer and gold dust. Underpin fragility and make virtue of not concealing it. The objects are made stronger and more valuable by repair. The below uses this as a metaphor for the body.

*Published in Jonah in 2019.
*Republished in Ginosko in 2026.

Time is a writer

of little diffidence – a sprite

who draws on everything:

the wrinkle next to the eye,

the fading of the light,

into an artistry of go(l)d --

wabi sabi, loving imperfections

we ponder.

Oh, the strange splendor of despair,

I’d despair, for as we age

we all age, and

these marks, a kintsugi

upon fragility, whose fragility is

persistence.

Such is the dialogue

Our faces hold, sans word

In the more golden years.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
