A Philosophy on Death

Published in HEART in 2018
Why do we philosophize about death, as if we’re going to figure it out? It doesn’t make much sense to agonize over a question we will never answer (And if you think your armchair contains the opening of some revelatory window, perhaps found staring in the abyss of the cup holder, abyss to it all, then you’ve only ever been sleeping in that chair…which you probably foolishly see as an analogy for death anyway… For If sleep is an analogy to death, so is stretching). Why do we entertain the reaper, the host – hostage, who will invite us all into some concept of eternal “home.” On that thought: reaper, an analogy oddly bucolic and by extension peaceful, picturing as I do anyway some Elysian field of perfectly lined crops …who nod their heads in the wind in agreement with whatever daydream we have (And why reaper? Do people hate reaping? And agricultural in general doesn’t reflect life; it is life)… Yet it doesn’t make sense to not agonize over death either (to instead get lost in between some ellipses). At the very least, the appropriate time to do it seems to be before you’re dead… So shall we crack open tomes of old, equating the dust from their pages to be what we be- come? Let our eyes prick away at Epicure’s syntax to find some whisper of truth in-between the letters? Act like some Virgil in tennis shoes, guiding our admirers to the truth in verse? We can pick any name for death. But what will we find? Maybe this is the source of all madness. This -- Why do we ask questions that we won’t find the answer to. Why do we Ask these to nobody in particular. Because (and you will see that ‘because’ Is as good of an answer as you’re going to get Because – see this recursiveness – Is as good of an answer as there is) The signifier/linguistic can only answer death. So saith the word, but not of God. It is a word without Capitalization, for there is no breakbetweenitselfandthenextwordletalone Capitalization. How dare we name it? How dare we think That There Is An Answer --which is to say Which is to say…
