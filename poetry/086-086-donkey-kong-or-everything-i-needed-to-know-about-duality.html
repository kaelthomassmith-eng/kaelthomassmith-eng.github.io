Poem 86

Donkey Kong; Or Everything I Needed to Know About Duality

*Published in Call me [Brackets] in 2020
Journal / archive ↗
We are each of us villains,
a barrel full of id,
throwing our anger
on a downward ladder,
but maybe we’re also heroes --
at least in tie that good bids.
But perhaps, we’re just monkeys,
either way, eating the banana by.
