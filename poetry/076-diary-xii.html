Diary XII

Published by Chewers by Masticadores in 2026 · Published/online version · Journal
She put the entire city of Chicago in her suitcase, and -- found traffic to be too bad to go anywhere, so she drank a cup of joe, and shared it with the World, which turned its cheek. She saluted lampposts in her hometown for being themselves and bathed in everything they said, albeit with her clothes on, draping each thought in a type of silk. She bathed in those lampposts’ light, bonus genius, Eros with a sexual experience, which she likened to -- not ‘liked’ -- the conversion of some martyr. She thought of herself as cut from a block (and your cynical self knows which one), nescient of the world, except that she provided the perimeter along which to cut. She had bitchy words spill out of her mouth with a vibrato like a sad guitar, and she channeled her inner noun into some damn mystery – if comedy can ever be politically wrong, No one can be sure, but every face she makes has a mustache. She knew absolutely that her words made a livid kind of sense, but her lips betrayed her with perissology, rambling until she wondered how many syllables the average English poem trapped inside itself. And wondered how many could be set free. She saluted the city she carried with her -- scoliosis bending her to bow with “reverence” to the city that tried to carry her instead.
