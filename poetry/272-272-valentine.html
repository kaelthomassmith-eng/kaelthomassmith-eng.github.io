Valentine

*Published in Outsider Poetry in 2018

a heart, not so very distant

a heart, not so very distant

a heart, not so very distant

a heart not so very distant

a heart, NOT so very distant

a heart, not so very distant

a heart, not so very distant…

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
