20 Love Notes to Tokyo

Published in whimperbang 2016
(1) Happiness On the broken leg o’ the sun 25-hours a day (2) A child’s eye unblends Trees through a cloudy blanket From the mountain tops? (3) While a fine rice-hat Orbits the (shadowed) head Of a man’s blind spot. (4) And rickshaws roll through A forest of temples Of human nature. (5) Ideas bleed out Through the black blood of a brush: Calligraphy (6) A lone cricket sings - Jūhachiban And the earth shakes its haunches (7) A mass of tongues Clicking phonetic lines - Music of the country (8) Romance Is a seven letter word But one kanji (9) For in winter’s tux I want to marry a’ plum blossom With whispered vows (10) Only man can grow A sordid mushroom Twenty stories tall (11) Cocoon of tomorrow Bursts forth industry Pachinko’d humans (12) Like vertical bridges - Signatures On the iris o’ my eye (13) Mask of geisha Contains every emotion In one expression (14) Miniature - A smile the size of an atom - Dolls. (15) Doors of woodblock cuts - (Pre-cut) Of Ten thousand leaves (16) God juggled The sun, the earth, and the moon In a cosmic circus (17) Sakura Drips from the third In an aerial puddle (18) Vacation: Counting a hot spring’s bubbles Is godly work. (19) A squished pond shows Ghosts of all That look into it? (20) Nihon: rings a sound Of a thousand neon suns, Eternally
