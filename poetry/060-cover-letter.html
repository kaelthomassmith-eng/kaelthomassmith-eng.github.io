Poem 60 of 291

Cover Letter

Published in Rush in 2026
To whom it may concern (never write who) I’m submitting the attached to apply -- Does not every word read somehow imply “Thank you for your consideration” too? -- I give a page of cordiality, With intentions devilishly padded to sell my individuality, but I hear that’s how man is formatted -- Like this document, in a preferred font -- the Times New Roman of man’s attitude to fit within your office’s entente the prime candidate for your servitude: I am nothing beyond a white, pressed shirt, Holding down my soul well into the dirt.
