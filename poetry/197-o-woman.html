O, Woman

Published in Alpha Female, 23 March 2020
O, woman thy airy ways have -- but stop! You must be green of reading po’tic sop That’s written about virginal stares blate -- Is such not dropped from some much thickened pate? But soft -- to write a poem on writing not Is still but movement of quill’s doting jot. As silver doubloons of negative flirt -- My words are traitors! Me the fool, they blurt Now take, fee simple, your leave sans Envoi. And ride your linguistic Ba-yard in joy To even lusher meadows of your own. (An apology, this, for decades thrown) So quit that heart (it beats along too fast) And read instead the words your eyne saw last.
