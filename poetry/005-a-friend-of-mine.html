A Friend of Mine

Published in Anima in 2020 / *Republished in Local Train Magazine, possibly 2026?
I went and saw a friend of mine We went outside to play In fields of haughty woodbine, Sun cresting at midday But soon the shadows all around Had made the dusk the queen And my friend made a soft sound. (Do you know what I mean?) “A spirit,” he said, pointing out Some tall and grassy knoll. “A certain part, a hot bout Of the past’s forlorn toll.” So heel to heel we walked along Humming a gentle song. But as I stared and looked ‘round My friend, nowhere, was he found.
