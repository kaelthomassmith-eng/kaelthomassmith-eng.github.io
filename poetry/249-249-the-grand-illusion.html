Poem 249

The Grand Illusion

*Published in Pennsylvania Literary Journal in 2018
Broken triggers on a leash held short,
Still tug out of our burnt hands.
Only the people seen in the cityscape,
even exist to the boss,
who plays these foosball tables uncouthly.
 
We’re suffering from post-traumatic stress disorder
with straw dolls.
Somewhere in the world lies a conglomeration
Of all things bet and won.
 
But I don’t think this qualifies for it.
And I really don’t think the tone that was taken
was appropriate for corporate humor.
 
This is an Orwell novel translated into Esperanto --
with footnotes.
The world’s Order is ‘senating’ is,
writing a thousand novels where nothing fucking happens.
 
Typing them up with monkey fingers,
we fell asleep in the middle of a theory.
It’s a testament to the philosophy of roulette,
to the impossibility of nothing yet.
