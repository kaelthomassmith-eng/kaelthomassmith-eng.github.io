Nothing but a Fancy
*Published in Ithaca in 2016

To all poems but a fancy
A blot of ink upon the mind

Of Mimir’s well come’t dry

Where ever-word is spelt new:

The lambs of time’s misty dew

Falling stars of curtained hearts
One heart beat away from a

Symphony of mental sprites brood.

If you cannot be read

The Dasein is the ‘timely dead

Miss the sun with but a blink

Like a masonic tower crumbled

Ere construction eynes itself:

A cliché unique to two

Yet always a thing new.

In empty jars store a boon:

Lost kisses from tomorrow

Like warning signs lit up too soon.

And in the land of naught but itself

You will be loved in barren wealth.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
