People Walk But Everywhere

Published in Badlands in 2017
a dark autumnal night I found not a soul not a sigh around people walk but everywhere, but they don’t walk here, not here like a home with a solid eye shut, not here -- where being is what being’s not with death’s fingerprint impressed upon, lo bending your eyes somewhere anon to a winking star a courier an inky well of a’ worrier, as in a low dell a taper fights a strife -- he said let me die a young man’s death but live an old man’s life… but said it with nary a breath let me know let me ‘no’ why man asked why the sun and moon share the same seam.
