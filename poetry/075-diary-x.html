Diary X

Published by Verse Virtual, September 2018; Republished by Home Planet News Online · Journal
I kept the sunshine in my back pocket, which she pruned with her eyelids' trim. Though all smiles – I could not talk to her. I kept the sunshine in my back pocket, Every smile partitioned out like a piano key, The prince of sundials, has now retired. I kept the sunshine in my back pocket, which she pruned with her eyelids' trim.
