Do You Remember God?

published in Visions with Voices in 2014-2019?; republished in Time of Singing much later, around 2026?
her body in its movement made the argument: I am beautiful. but I was too shy to look away from a myriad of onlookers, who passed by in times overtkill. The problem is it’s so easy to fall in love, and almost impossible to fall out. I don’t want to speak for her, but I wish She would speak for me, because I wrote all my memories for the judge.
