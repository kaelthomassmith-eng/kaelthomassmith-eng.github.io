Poem 32 of 291

Bathtub

Published in Alpha Female in 2019.
1) Meticulous droplets fill a dirty tub held together by a ring of filth, a matrimonial altar of the unspeakable -- no light for the damned soap king, who lives or dies by the moon, swirling down in repetition. 2) Meticulous, the drops fill a basin bound by a wedding ring of filth – naked, here is the bridal bed. to consummate the tallow king. Who loves according to the timetable, the womanly name for a woman. 3) Meticulous droplets fill up a tubby with a ring of siolfor -- crows-feet of filth mark it kingly. No light for the fatty ruler, who wanes and waxes on the habits that drain. 4) Meticulous goes the drippy-droppy, filling the fat porcelain tummy, bonding the marriage of filth and cleanliness -- for the doomed soap king, loves a Mayan goddess, who falls into the drain of habits. 5) Drops meticulously fill a basin dirty, Anno Domino – we are together, on a second-place throne, a kingdom over stains, that ebbs and flows with brushing all our fears down the drain. 6) Meticulous tears from Anahita, fall into a specie-like chalice offered upon an altar of dirt. Dies the cult -- dies the clean soul. cast in the shadow of noon while the milky way falls into an abyss.
