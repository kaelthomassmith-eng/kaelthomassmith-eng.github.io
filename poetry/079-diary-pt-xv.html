Poem 79 of 291

Diary, pt. XV

Published in The Collidescope, 24 May 2020
Consuming a high-fat philosophy diet (a polyhistor with an intellect so great his shadow had a shadow), I went to Paris, just to parody it. But there is no charming grammatical error to woe my beaus, no comma splice in which to hide the thoughts I truly want to say. It wasn’t Man who fell; it was the sentence.
