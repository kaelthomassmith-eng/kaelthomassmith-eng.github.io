POTS AND PANS

*Published in Lullwater Review in 2018

Peas in a pod,

softer hands to harder hearts,

the unspoken word

sinks into a silver trough of waste.

For the wife of poor Ulysses

holds an arched pose

bowing to nothing to arride,

instead to dive,

soaking ideas to scrub them out,

this poor siren

sings herself into a trance,

sings realization away

while as periscope in this ocean of bubbly goop

her hands turned into a corpse.

Wash was a four letter word,

measured out by an oven timer,

and black pots may come clean

but never honest.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
