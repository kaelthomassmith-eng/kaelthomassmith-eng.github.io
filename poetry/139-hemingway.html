Hemingway

Published in Two Thirds North in 2019.
The day was as short as a Hemingway sentence, which (in itself) is a metaphor for life. The day was short, and it was a metaphor for life. The old --- the old man knew that he was dead, but the world did not accept it; the people did not accept it. And the ocean rolled its ‘r’s as he sat at the bow, raping the boat’s hull – but together they lumbered on – his love and he. The day was short, And it was a metaphor for his life.
