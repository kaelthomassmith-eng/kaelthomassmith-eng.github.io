The Dawn Kills

Published in Chewers by Masticadores in 2018 · Published/online version · Journal
The dawn kills. The light destroys just as Lucifer does, who was Heosphoros (Greek: Morning Star…look it up). the – (definition) destroys. In fact, Definitions (denotations) do destroy. In fact -- in point of fact, the ‘the’ destroys everything it precedes (possibilities, algorithms, truths…), basically the plural itself, by throwing away possibilities, beauty hidden under a curtain cruel. Your knowledge is the better for -- Your knowledge is better.
