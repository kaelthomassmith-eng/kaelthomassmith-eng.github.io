Femme Fatale

*Published in Wilderness House in 2026
She’s feminism
In a dinged tin can --
 
sold in groceries
of overstocked shelves;
 
In the shadow of
split infinitives.
 
She takes her slow drags
off cigarette stubs.
 
In the shadow of
An arched lamppost
 
She mixes small talk
with discarded ash
 
She is the whole world’s
Other. reflected
