Poem 97

*error message*

*Published in Zombie Logic Review in 2016
Gently tip-toe with the one-toothed deer (envy)
in hallowed halls with ghosts in night-gowns. (sloth)
Words make love in the bed of our minds (lust)
into a sky of cookies and crème (gluttony)
 
From a melted Van Gogh (O! Angry sun) (wrath)
a smile of thirty rungs can twist a lake (pride)
out of a scarf of skin, a dead Plowman (cawl)(greed)
mourning cypress’s murdered sin un-begot.
