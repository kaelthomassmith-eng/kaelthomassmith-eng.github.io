Poem 12 of 291

A Reflection on Nature: The Arve in Summer

Published in Inwood Indiana in 2018. · Published/online version · Journal
Better to understand a river By understanding how I understand a river I must step back from the bank of thought And let it flow It’s not a cross or censor Any more than nature’s confidential Its hidden nooks hold secrets That the wind gossips about to the trees That the Arve baubles about to the rocks To the passerby’s of lee In these rooms of God’s house Turned inside out like a dirty garter Nothing is ever lost Let it flow into the unconscious tombs Of the mind, through awful scenes Shadowed in a permanent midnight In the many locked rooms of God’s house Lie the relics of an elder time, Who caterwaul about like bigotry And let it flow.
