Cento from Google’s ‘I’m Feeling Lucky’

Published in Technoculture in 2018 · Journal
Despite an inscription in English at the bottom left corner I began work on this doodle celebrating his work (many retain their dark pitch-log facades and take a few deep breaths). The place is not to be confused: dive in to view the world at street level even if folded from the same basic plan. Generosity is contagious: the whole world, in your hands.
