Poem 135 of 291

Have you ever?

Published in BlogNostics, 2018; Republished in Galway Review, 16 May 2026
1 Have you ever cut your words with a splice by the use of a renegade comma, have you split the infinitive so nice, as to blankly write linguistic drama? 2 Your verse was written in novitiate; Do you wonder ‘if a passive voice mess was written by you?” – a’ Midas vitiate. Do you think your errors are somehow less? 3 Or haphazardly using an em dash -- you need training how to hold your pencil if you would punctuate by means so rash, not only use adverbs, but the -ly kill! 4 Did you ever – I can ever exclaim -- Reflect on your poor language, that you maim!
