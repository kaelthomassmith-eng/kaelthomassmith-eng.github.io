Buy This Poem

Published in Chewers by Masticadores in 2026 · Published/online version · Journal
Could you imagine what it would be like to buy something? To own it, to have it be actually be yours? You could buy a tree. Who’s to say you couldn’t buy three. Your ecstasy must be… indescribable, only fitting in a Seussian-esque spree. Could you imagine if parts of the world were simply…yours? That within your empty palm you held things that weren’t even there? You’d be a magician, nay – some sort of gibbous god who could stand on a land while your feet were raised up. Could you dream of buying something not even real itself? You could buy rights for instance -- probably the biggest and longest Freudian slip of the law. Or could you imagine owning a single word – so that that word is yours and no one else could use it anymore, at least not without your permission. You could buy the sky – You could buy a feeling, so long as you imagined the law as a phallic plaything -- not crass but overtly there… insofar as delusion hasn’t been regulated or for sale. Could you imagine owning yourself Your time Your family Your life Your happiness Your choices? Could you imagine buying yourself? You’d probably wait for a discount.
