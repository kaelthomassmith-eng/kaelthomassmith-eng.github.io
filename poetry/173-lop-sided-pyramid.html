Lop-Sided Pyramid

Published in Zombie Logic Review 2013-2018
Once, Burnt Umber Carved a story In Hieroglyphics of faith, Including the unforgotten saviors-o’-men, Utilizing th’ concrete personification of Fibonacci numbers (Population architecturally distributing manufactured superstition unfathomably blueprinted).
