Poem 77 of 291

Diary XIII

Published by whimperbang in 2016
I fear the world may not be able to be revised. I fear that I’ve learned how to fear with age. I fear this tick, but then fear I’m only a conglomeration of ticks. I fear both having and not having an audience. I fear not only the word, but the eternal structure in which it lies. I fear I’ve sold my cash for too much. I fear there’s a limit to time. I fear the man next to the pauper wants to be him. I fear the world only looks good on paper. (I fear you will not look). I fear my lunch will not go down in history (like Breton’s). I fear I / you / we only think in footnotes. I fear you only want to see similarity. I fear line fourteen. I fear nothing.
