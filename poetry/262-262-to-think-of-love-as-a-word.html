To think of love as a word

*Published in Visions with Voices in 2018

And

I just stepped into her house

on the threshold of the future --

a nyaff in a mind of whiskey,

but anent nothing but our sighs.

And

a haunt of an hollowed spoon

can bring her tears to my mouth

where I can drink of those bitters

to the future sins within us both.

And

her eyes fluttered in an endless circle

as I stood firmly in the now:

so old I can’t remember my first kiss;

so young I can’t remember my last.

And

I only learned the word

“nacreous” to steal sympathy

From those very eyes

For what you call “always.”

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
