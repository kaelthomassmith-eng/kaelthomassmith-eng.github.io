Poem 140 of 291

Hero

Published in Academy of the Heart in 2013-2018
The earth revolved like a’ unbent hula-hoop, Their dialogue a’ bag of found-words had scooped: T’was two lovers twixt a phone and such things; Two mindsets drawn from the image of rings. Ver’ly, she could have palled; she could have called: Her’s was a voice kept close on a tied string, A little tot o’ dirtied knees n’ broken wing (The wind spoke more in a’ lazy chair lolled). With winking raise the mast in an armed hoist So that this bard can catch a’ poor man’s wind free To sail so lost in a’ land of mimicry While deafened towards some limed inner voice. The picture was: he loved her; she loved he, But neither o’ them too solidly drawn be.
