Star Fox; Or, Everything I Needed to Learn About Race

Published in Call Me [Brackets], Issue 4, Spring 2020
It’s the truth that justice is only ever rogue, a heavy-handed attempt to subdue this veritable zoo. For in the fabric of space itself seems to be an Andross – a villain, who to protect the planet experiments with genetics -- Eugenics – in which prejudice is disembodied until it’s a floating head and the visible hand of capitalism. And the only thing that can stop this hand’s death grip may be a rumbling pack of justice – those who in the air shake the ground to its core. Enter the Inari fox -- he who becomes a hero by presumption of one kind or another, dodging debris within a hare’s breath with barrel rolls balletic. Animals are not man, nor puppets; we should not denigrate – Hate can soar beyond the falcon, Dive deeper than the toad But (together we stand) And freedom soars on the Arwing.
