Me

Published in Sincerely Magazine in 2018. / Republished in Ginosko in 2026 · Journal
I think of an idea split into two characters (from what stage-play, I know not): Me, of two letters: ‘m’ and ‘e’ -- the latter the most common used letter in the English language, such that I -- me -- am a part from everyone around me. In duo… these letters two: unsure— if this is a connection or a separation. For duality is: all that is wrong with the world (but maybe not), shifting in its sound like a continental divide of history. And that ‘M.’ Phonologically like your two lips kissing themselves. How can this not be self-care? And more so, a historical letter that Histrio states as follows: based on an Egyptian hieroglyph that looks to me ( to m e ) like waves endlessly flowing across the page, saying all things are transitory, even me. And maybe ‘M’ is just that – a memento mori, that will stab me to relish with its jagged edges, an act which states: that the world (those e’s perhaps) will follow me past my termination in ever- increasing strides, thousands if you view it as a number, a number from those romans who stole it. Or perhaps, it’s the empty space that constitutes the letters, and we exist in nothing but the kern, a series of footsteps, moving like a dancer hoping to hit a step early. Or maybe the meaning only lies in the inhale taken to say the word, an emptiness made: Together. They can be the duality of men in one man – Me? (chopping off letters like gangrenous limbs, whose amputee may be a duality of duality ad infinitum) …but is this any longer me?
