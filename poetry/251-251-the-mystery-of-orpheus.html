The Mystery of Orpheus

*Published in aois21 in 2016 (I think)

Please --

Advise me how to wake up

(possibly in the morning)

How is it done (with skill or caution)?

I've searched in stale literature,

possibly packed with asbestos,

to find some stolid emotions.

Yawns are like clay --

molded with the cauliflowered hand of time

and not rated on a growing Likert scale --

But rising more awkwardly like a bird with wings clipped.

And the status-quo is already a sing-song relic,

Of what you call the “now.”

And "Hold on" is but an island,

navigating a 'what' in full rotation

(and the rest is black).

So in this bed:

I don’t know how to get up.

there's metaphorical cocaine on a nose protruding --

it's Pinocchio in a whirlpool of know-it-allism,

wishing it all could be real.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
