LIFE

Published in Zombie Logic Review in 2013-2018
The story of life is depressing: xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx… And then you die. “S/He saved…” “S/He lied…” “S/He tried…” Whatever. You know what follows Past the remou of tradition: Like a bout o’ piblokto for the cold-hearted Made of a bonsella of words. So why narrativize it? (Maybe life is a poem) If not to modify the end: To end the end by an end…
