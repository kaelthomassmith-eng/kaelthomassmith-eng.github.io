Winter Afternoon

Published in EGOPhobia in 2019
I am writing a poem because it’s winter, and I am inside. I don’t want to Right now, and it shows – “Proving” my point by showing this poor muddle -- better than the telling words, flowery as they could be, tattling in their own way, and reveling in my own circle of complaints.
