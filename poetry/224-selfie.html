Selfie

Published in BlogNostics in 2019
Do I deck myself out with expressions not mundane? For kept in my back-pocket a juicy Apple of new temptation makes a narcissist of my eye’s socket. My sight along a path of sens’al stones, through a digital looking glass, it roams, picking rocks from out its shoes till some guise is seen, argued through an orgy of lies. Who could believe this shiny Iago? These previous ten shots deleted go -- for they cast me not as I will to see, but just how my boxed captor depicts me. So I’ll snap more shots, doubt what I perceive, until I capture me as I believe.
