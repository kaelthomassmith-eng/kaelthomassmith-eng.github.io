Oyster

Published in Modern Literature in 2018 · Published/online version · Journal
I got lost inside an echo in my head in a thousand fonts offsetting, and on my ship voyaging so slim, I sailed on thoughts closed up like oysters who hid only sand. Her face was like eye music -- a mooter for my past, and I’m mixing holes together in the stew of the long night, as clouds make nature’s rarissima. She said, “son you look bad -- from all the ruling mad,” tracing the lines of my frown she read my nimbose clouds, a metopomancy with her index, But I couldn’t wait by, assiduously pronouncing “hope” -- for the lady filled me up with loaded die and lies (since I’ve been loving more). So only I said lightly: “Sometimes, I can give a little grin, wouldn’t change it if I could, but sometimes isn’t always,” and my eyelids broke new ground.
