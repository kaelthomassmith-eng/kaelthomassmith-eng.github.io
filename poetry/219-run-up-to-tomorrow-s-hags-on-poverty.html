Run Up to Tomorrow’s Hags (On Poverty)

Published in 100 subtexts, Issue 45
Run Up to tomorrow’s hags and split your ideas in two, in front of the already hollowed head of some monster with a title, who while you kick around this old dirty still is waiting in a perfect babble, in which this King has stolen all you own and replaced it with a jeer. Sound is stepping up the long stairway as you laugh at your own funeral.
