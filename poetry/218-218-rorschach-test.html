Poem 218

Rorschach Test

*Published in 100 subtexts in 2019-2026
Ten amorphous blobs of my cherry thoughts
form an army of monochrome etches,
bits and splotches that float like a U-F-O,
refracting perspectives of the mellow --
such is the language of the weathervane,
of two souls who are petaliferous
in how they break the levee on taxed thoughts;
a music box Pegasaded my breath:
communication is a two-way street
with traffic, and these thoughts will hover over us
until we get suntans from their shadows.
