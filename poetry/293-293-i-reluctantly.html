I, reluctantly

*Published in Eckleburg, 27 November 2026.

I.
I, reluctantly
stepped
into the sliver of a phrase.

I looked to the left.
And I looked to the
Right while everybody’s body bowed,

You looked
Innocent–
ly at me. And I took
offense. It was your fault not mine.

I argued myself out
of heaven,
And sprayed your knowledge
into my eyes.

II.
We could kill
love –
in fact, I’m pretty certain we eventually will,
so let’s try to live this day to the fullest,
even if
we haven’t lived enough days
to know what that means.

Do we want our days to be crowded
until they can’t move forward
like an urban bottleneck?
I guess like most things
it beats loneliness.
Or maybe we should try to dress down and
marry the sunset.

We’d make an interesting pair,
lying down with one another
and the ceremony will be
short,
but I will be inundated with the best of men and wishes.

And we can live that day full
carpe diem --
a depressing phrase that
forgets fullness.

For, stick with me here,
we will capre noctem,
and double our desires
compared to false prophets,

and if we pluck that
concept clean like a bone,
then death -- like the day --
will only set softly,
and we won’t be alone.
