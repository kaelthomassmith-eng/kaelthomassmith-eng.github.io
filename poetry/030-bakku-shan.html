Bakku-Shan

Published in Evening Street Review, Number 29, Mid-spring 2021; Republished in Galway Review, 16 May 2026
I don’t know what your beauty is, but – Ooo, that snatched me. I was entranced by only a frame of you. (Do you judge this? I’m not alone, and all people should have this freedom – ) Bakku-Shan, meaning a beauty when viewed from behind for hotness from the back is the definition of optimism – I think. And if you still feel bad (despite my best intentions) then know we never really judge a person, only parts. And it’s just like Dali said while putting drawers on the venus de milo: the beauty of the femme lies in its mystery. It -- an object, I understand – and I don’t mean to offend, but from behind, your form’s an ideal of the moment, your bureau is filled with whispers of secrets I want to know. This poem is my secret, a wink you can take with you wherever you may be on your way to go, or throw away, as you go like the dance your hips suggests.
