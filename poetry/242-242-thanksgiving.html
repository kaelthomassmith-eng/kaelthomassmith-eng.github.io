Poem 242 of 291

Thanksgiving

Published in Pennsylvania Literary Journal in 2018
I don’t think things through --

“I love you.”


The last month’s hypocrisy

is now mimicked in my mouth--

the weather

recalled in my mind like a trauma,

a season unseasoned in its fickleness,

a Baltic habit coming out once again…

well, at least I tried.


The threat of snow flurries

didn’t need to be stated.

And the kiddos laughed outside

like fairies dancing on broken glass.


We walked along houses,

strewn like dirty dishes along a sink,

hosting what will not be the talk of tomorrow.

I only know what is the least present,

stretched along a fathomless mind.

And in the dime of the eye,

I see the world as a duplicate self --

an ambush of shadows,

an ambush of copies,

an ambush of the little things,

an ambush of my very own voice --

to speak but of itself

but only to matter in the crook of another’s ear.


Why ask where we were going?

“Was it to be that road less trod on?” You said.

 I said back, “I don’t want to know.”

(hell, I don’t even know the road’s name),

and now my spirit’s diagonal.


To enter

into the humid air of the house:

the wind’s heavy-hanging

scrotum hits the face,

and a familial swarm mills about

like unpaid proletariats.


We heated the bird,

but not the conversation --

and were hugged by the many doors: our table, our dinner…

The crowd was possessed by the holidays --

but we were already taken.



We couldn’t forget that outside

all the din was actually true.

Rape the body of the earth.

Rape the bodies that hoist up the earth --

pillage, violate, obfuscate it all

(I sigh)

until the father was proven right

…until the disrespect is overloaded to the level of the linguistic

…until I drown in a spew of synonyms.

All accurate.

None emotional.


This is what we’re thankful for,


so the only thing I could do was worship

my body spread out.

The only thing that mattered

was how much the physical was

mimicking my analogies.


I had put the pencil to my head

in a suicidal motion,

brought it down with a crash on the paper.

My oh my -- let the word die,

drowned in a sea of white lies.


So I travel on foot to your heart --

my shoes licking the pavement --

to wherever I housed my memories,

a place as empty as this house is full,

ever looking for something there,

ever stuck in the hallways of yearbook,

ever lost in the maze thereof,

ever waiting for the coming dawn,

but never the chosen one.


The sun

lit only the surfaces of things;

more so, it lit only the surfaces

that I have touched.


I drew a rough draft of God,

discovering more of your face --

every time I look at it --

in every line I follow with an imprisoned eye,

studying whatever I thought I saw

balder and bolder.


Our mittens were cuffs in the cold November day,

saying we never had to leave

...but that’s not what we actually believe.


What they thought were their morals were disgusting.

What the day represented was two-faced,

yet we shouted across the lawn

“I am yours,”

like some alleged movie cliché.


I want to make language dance like a wanton girl

as seen in the eyes of a lonely prude.

I want my analogies to dance around the world

on the lips of any brief lover.


But it wouldn’t show.

It would only tell a cardinal sin

(“Haha,” we’d say -- how funny is it

that we only remember the seven sins?)

But it only came out:


“I love you.”

See, I always said I was a fool.
