Poem 64 of 291

Cycles

Published in Spank the Carp in 2018; Republished in Academy of the Heart and Mind somewhere around 2018; Republished in Ginosko in 2018 · Journal
Wound in a circle from a hasty bit of work, I couldn’t drive myself to leave the absence you left behind, and so I -- I, who had been shaved so thin, to the span of a breath, as thin as this twiggy character -- I peer into the past with an involuntary wink, and wonder how I never saw it before. That here, you – you refuse to leave.
