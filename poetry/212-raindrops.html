Raindrops

Published in Technoculture in 2018 · Journal
Rain’s in her eyelashes; I-lashes, of a lost identity baking -- prepped for two hours sans a breath (and sprinkled with an argot). Into the fair-held cornea, she simply walked a hard footing like Elijah’s upward stroll into those pearly gates of old. We were out of our heads and doing perfectly fine -- looking back at ourselves with eyelids borrowed secondhand. So pantomime the fall of Troy and garnish the ‘everyday’ like a salad of a lost king’s tomorrow, and don’t let the sun be itself: For the rain soaked up the sunlight like a sponge saturated with Minerva’s tears, which through the drama of half a’ page is pushed beyond its own curtains. Then, I-lashes lifted a square temple, and her thoughts pitter-pattered into the city of whispered veils ‘for an effigy of James Joyce and like. I ran faster than my thoughts’ growth into the world of language benign. And you only – stuck in the rain – Allowed ‘me’ to be ‘mine.’
