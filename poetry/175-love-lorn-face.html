Love-Lorn Face

Published in Founders Favourite in 2016
The lovelorn face makes its own weather cold Exhaling cirrus clouds of misty sighs That float around the topsy-turvy world Like a bog at Michaelmas by in by And the monsoon to leak from out her eyne Lids close at quarter-moon in lover’s pine And the thunderous cries shake this poor keg Like timorous Atlas on a broken leg Come morning as the sinus pressures change Sniffles blow a shaky Borean wind From out nostrils to the hardened grange As cold as when a coatless Lear grinned For the forecast with a climate heading Is like th’ lover in a Monkey’s Wedding.
