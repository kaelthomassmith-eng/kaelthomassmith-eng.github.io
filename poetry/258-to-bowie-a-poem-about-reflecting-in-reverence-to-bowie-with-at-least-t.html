To Bowie

Published in Door is a Jar, Issue 16, Fall 2020 (journal issue page: Door is a Jar)
...and your dreams are so close to your face that they gave you a black eye. (We sat down on the couch and kept falling, looking for Major Tom – our drinks rye). The glam stars hung loosely above us, where man made a cliché of god’s beauty, so turn the record on its spindle but only if its rotation is a rebellious duty. Sitting, we passed upon the stairs to meet a pretty thing, who only gingerbreaded up her eyes, sans gaze, so that we existed together lonely. We died with our mouths open from the memory of a free will, before the hologramic machine took our ashes and made our fame kill. Let’s soar my friend to the greatest heights with a wink and a satanic grin. How sad if the planets above didn’t dance with beautiful and feigned indifference.

A poem about reflecting in reverence to Bowie, with at least ten allusions to him (not readily obvious) that is meant to be read through the codex of those allusions.
