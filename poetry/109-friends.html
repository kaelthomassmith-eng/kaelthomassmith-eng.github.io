Friends

Published in DoveTales, Winter Edition, Issue II, February 2020
Where do the regular people grow? I want to pick one like a petal from off a quatrefoil, whose stem stretches back home, in a field of malicious openness. All this is foreign to me, who only strolls into this field like a breeze. The frowning of the sky at my gait, a j’accuse of my unwelcome self, is an adoption of a bygone tradition. How to be a regular person, I know not. My stalk is but a mere adaptation of sorrow. I’ve lived my whole life in this land, But still I’m only visiting.
