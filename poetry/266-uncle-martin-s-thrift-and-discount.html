Uncle Martin’s Thrift and Discount

Published in Adelaide in 2012-1017 · Journal
Winter winds can only tell lies Those charlatans of haughtily Refined words. Tell them to kiss The sunrise and I and you and Anyone who surmises a natural Riddle translated from Helvetica Will eat the tinniest words you’ve Never Seen. But alas, allow That along alighted fields Of the most natural study That was ever rehashed: Modern comes again.
