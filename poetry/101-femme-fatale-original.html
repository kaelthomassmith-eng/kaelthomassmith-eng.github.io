Femme Fatale (original)

Not published but the original version
She’ll smile like a knife, cutting short your plot -- ever in the time of what’s “now-gone-by.“ But her fair face is nought but a vizard from an old Greek play Wrapped in on itself. Older than any young person should be, she shows her true age like a gambler’s hand. N’ artificial fire has lit up her voice; heels sound her coming (the same as going) To plant a lip-sticked kiss with dual wings ever on the cheek of the chosen lamb -- For like Hallmark cards, her love’s plagiarized: a solitude of two in booths of blue (Ever a baby one would be with her: ever a kept pet but never a name). As that burning voice scalds like the coffee newly poured from out her lips to the floor -- Poison in a mug ringed with a stretched ouroboros of red lip-liner. She’s feminism In a dinged tin can -- sold in groceries of overstocked shelves; In the shadow of split infinitives. She takes her slow drags off cigarette stubs. In the shadow of An arched lamppost She mixes small talk with discarded ash She is the whole world’s Other. reflected in a diner’s spoon, now so freshly licked. She is the shadow’s medicated tics of a simple plot deterministic.
