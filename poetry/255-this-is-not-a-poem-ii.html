This is not a poem II

Published in Modern Literature, 5 September 2020; Republished in Agony Opera, February 2021
Ceci n'est pas une – I don’t speak French, I’m not even casually acquainted with it really, so it may Be. Full stop. More so, I don’t smoke -- said the man to the man, (himself) But I do know how to lie. This godwottery will only grow, can ever know the treachery of life.
