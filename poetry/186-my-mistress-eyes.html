My Mistress’ Eyes

Published in Written Tales in 2020; Republished in Rush in 2026
A Sonnet My mistress’ eyes on black morn darker be than the sun amid versed tragedy. And beneath her eyes are deeply entrenched sad memories with tears sordidly drenched Her voice is not of songbird’s melody but like a crow call trying to be ‘Frenched’ While words of hers change like a wanton sea Below hair like gulf weed itself had clenched Her kiss the taste of strawberries shan’t know, But be of her own taste, something discrete And her gait may be such as drunkards go - Of stomp and stumble on unsoundly feet But of her faults – as you may see – to I None, but parts of aged love do descry.
