Poem 73 of 291

Depression 3 (a quatern)

Published in Prachya Review in 2019
They said it would all get better as I cried out to the thin world in a voice so hoarse that it could not even pierce your tough Mechlin heart I dug my own grave without hands -- they said it would all get better in words decked out in fine prosy. It didn’t and I didn’t, and -- Sleep does not obey the morning: no matter how loudly it crows. They said it would all get better, yet I whittle the hours away. I’ll tell you how the sad sun fell a ribbon at a time. Yet I dared look for the dusk only ‘cause they said it would all get better.
