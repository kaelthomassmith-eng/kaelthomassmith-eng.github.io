Marbling

Published in jmww in 2019
The final poem in this series, “Marbling” is in reference to Ebru, also known as paper marbling. Ebru is the Turkish tradition of painting on water, an act that creates beautiful (abstract) designs. The Ottomans used Ebru to decorate the spines of books. It was also used as a type of code – if the pattern on the spine was broken, then you’d know some pages were missing, i.e. someone intercepted the message.
