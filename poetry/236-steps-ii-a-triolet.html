Steps II (a triolet)

Published in Wise Owl in 2026
For still our steps move on and on, Marching to our wayward heartbeats, Keeping the tempo of the sun -- For still our steps move on and on Until the brittle break of dawn Taking the filth off the streets, For still our steps move on and on, Marching to our wayward heartbeats.
