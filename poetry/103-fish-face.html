Fish Face

Published in Harbinger in 2018; Slump* · Journal
“Through the curtain folds,” she said and laid in the softest part of grey light -- only playing with the topography of pocks. Her fingers, taught with sinews like shanks of meat, enclosed on his -- a grasp of strangulation -- the ‘love’ died. Then: her mouth fish-feeding on his with a taste cannibalistic. “Go.” Sea moans echoed up to the flooded chambers of his mind, it all moving slowly like the ocean floor; While in a purgatory of smells, limbs flailed like seaweed, On lumps on an unknown land, inflated on the windpipe’s moans -- all to win the gilded Oscar. And place it on the bed-stand.
