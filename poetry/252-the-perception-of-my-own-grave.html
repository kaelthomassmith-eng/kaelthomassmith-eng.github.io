The Perception of my Own Grave

Published in EgoPHobia in 2016
The perception of my own grave Is always a long thought. Death becomes pictorial, Held by the thistled frame of tall grass. This mountain at a’ given distance All the more clear becomes When the artist’s jiggling hand Drops the coarse brush in art’s depiction. It seems a shame that anything But weeds should form this shroud. Yet th’ joke’s calling this plot mine When worms form my makeshift diadem
