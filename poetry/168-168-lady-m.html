Lady M --

*Published in Technoculture in 2017

Lady Macbeth,

“who” spoken on a cold lip -- but halt!

A wind is lost in a castle: a whisp(er) of trees

with baby lips of soupy faces,

mouth too full of teeth.

Nature’s from the lim

along an abstract timeline.

“Out damn stain!” she said.

And the castle it cried for her.

And the wind it cried for her.

And the Melpomene mask it cried for her.

But her words were so thin

they had bulimia

Scrubbing her hand from

off her prints --

a water basin of tears. Self-survival

is the longest trick

to humanity’s whoring

licks.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
