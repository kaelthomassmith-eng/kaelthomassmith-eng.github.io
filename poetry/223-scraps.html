Scraps

Published in Torrid Lit in 2018 (with a different format than the one posted below, please include both versions)
I’m making Dreams out of what ever scraps I find. Mashing them together like A child’s Food, at Supper time. And my Achilles heel Lies in the crook of your eyes, Whose salty tonic with its secrets tucked away rage like an army in waiting. Here, I couldn’t show you the stars, But at least you could describe them I didn’t know I was starving Until I ate all of the stems.
