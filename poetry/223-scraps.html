Scraps

Published in Torrid Lit in 2018

I’m making

Dreams

out of whatever

scraps

I find. Mashing them

together like

A child’s

Food,

at

Supper time.

And my

Achilles heel

Lies

in the

crook

of

your

eyes,

Whosesalty

tonic

with its

secrets

tucked

away

ragelike an

army

inwaiting.

Here, I

couldn’t

show

you

the stars,

But

at least

you

coulddescribe

them

I

didn’t

know

Iwas

starving

Until

I

ate

all of

the

stems.
