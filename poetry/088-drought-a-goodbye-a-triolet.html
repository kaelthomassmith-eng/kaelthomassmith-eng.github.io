Drought: a goodbye (a triolet)

Published in Kreatif in 2026
The world’s proudest feature is her water, the fancy dress worn to show herself off, a fashion that’s fitting magna Mater. The world’s proudest feature is her water, so great it formed the whole of life’s blotter. Now, she has died; the heat her ware did quaff The world’s proudest feature is her water, the fancy dress worn to show herself off.
