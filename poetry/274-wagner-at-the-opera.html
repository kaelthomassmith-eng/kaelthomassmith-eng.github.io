Wagner at the Opera

Published in Collidiscope in 2017
Prelude F Night after night, at a lamppost’s height Euterpe’s charge in an armadillo shell, closed in a cavern of ethereal might, a hurricane of sound swells -- Did you take such a breath? Indoors, we gel to the music, of the indoor swirl (like a toilet bowl). B What to find, but a Lazarus of mind Fueling ideas of an extinct kind D# As I hold my sign of protest, I know ‘euphemism’ is a euphemism. From the mouth of the Goebbels of atheists flow Words. They are ours (a refraction – prismatic), and all along every inch of the fret move, piping aqua vitae to the Holy Land’s summit G# A day a day (for the sum total of nothing: "eine Handlung”) The seated took it. An aesthetic dossier of ear-ale drunk but not drunken. For him, the past is a screen that mocks ignorance, and I see relics of a present that could have been: season of trysts and colds (oh, Valkyries of Time die so an epic may play again). War didn’t begin here. Just the absence of harmony. Act 1 F ‘Wild Irish Maid’s’ --only exist on the tongues of those who tell tales. On every helm is a sailor of the coming day, while the North Atlantic sea moves like a laugh across the face of the earth. “I would it were rising to consume us.” B She found him like she left him, which is the story of a’ ‘mortally wounded’ life D# (And a wedding march is) played by a percussion of feet only -- walking down aisles of the temporal, the church was a fermentable sea, of cherubs n’ babes weeping ‘Morold,’ and one-handed claps. G# Hand in hand with a weary traveler, the Flying Dutchman portents the lower depth’s king, who wears a crown of albatrosses, seagulls and cloud surfers -- king to horrors sugarcoated. Look into the eyes; she can’t kill him Look into the eyes; the gloss is truth. In your eyes, would I fall in hate of you? Then, Kurwenal, the middle, will not contend a love made of brew – to not offend. Act 2 F ‘Too late – ‘ comes round the halls a Wallpaper voice to decorate the house of many rooms. Crowds like booze fill too many holes. In moist outdoors a logorrhea of minutes dance by in a reverbed churn, burning wills into a Neo-Grecian urn B Violence is the motif…for society is when dogs hunt for another party. D# (The point is I want what I shouldn’t want I want to not want because of the point that I cannot want want)…But this world is nothing but premonition. G# But hush -- ‘I exhaust my flame,’ like friends and funds. Marke the flame. Marke the dame. In a bedchamber – synonyms not lost for: ‘chamber’ -- hearts syncopate. Their limbs only indicate to new men on, drawn back to the castle. And so nothing discovered all: in coitus, spying eyes raise up the sun to a height it’s afraid of. Act 3 F Now, a chord of wind rents the sky -- a hasty scrawled rest: mimicry of a babe’s haunting cry, who at a mother’s young breast suckles the memory of fat-free milk. The rooster that caws in a tender valley of noon, notes the rod but not the staff. B So pipes eat themselves, in an Ouroboros when leitmotifs are leitmotifs. D# “I know this song…” The lover’s delirium walks with clenched hands -- remove Melot’s signature on vellum new, remove feet to talk in a sign language of heels. Or tears. G# Reign of obsequy in a sequined dress -- musical notes battle like a game of chess, awoke a queen of too tender thoughts, bought with a coupon: gratis, A nubile with a lipstick imprints “My -- ” He died. she was of legal age for the apocalypse, (But oh, Marke explained the poison n’ words) (But oh, mark how sad ‘tis when your name is synonymous with ‘goodbye’) Finale F On stage, a field of crosses knits the ground’s spouse who’s only grounds-kept by a field mouse. And mourners of weeds, brambles, and briers -- for nothing is nothing in shambles perspired -- form suits so primped and pressed for a marriage in death, for who dare not walk ‘neath Jacob’s ladder. B If an audience is those who watch, can an audience but obey? D# For Jacob’s ladder is a stairway to nothing to the German watching himself at a play. Should he cry, he cries for he, so say tea leaves with the motto of “anything in wolves’ clothing Is probably a sheep.” G# The sour aftertaste of false idols is like a morning kiss -- of lovers twixt. Glossolalia should sound like a scratched record -- a bohemian groan spit from a phonograph’s propaganda. Wagner sits counting the silences of his musical until “Liebstod,” as earthbound as a twice-born bird we’re separated from the herd. And there is where redemption lies.
