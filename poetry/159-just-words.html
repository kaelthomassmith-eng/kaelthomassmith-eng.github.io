Just words

Published in EgoPHobia in 2019; Republished in Fine Lines in 2019 · Journal
Truth is I don’t know if any of my experiences are real, or if I’m only stuck in a memory or two, which is essentially the footer to all I do. But -- can you tell me who I am from who I tell you? Or better to read me in the margins, in some text sous rature, for the truth is -- regardless of what either of us says -- full stop, I am a stranger unto reality, when so much of it exists outside of me. Can you tell me how you hurt the word? What you hate about yourself? How to be an outlaw for a dime? Who am I to judge what I remember?
