Nursery Rhyme

Published in Neologism in 2013?
When little boy blue jumped beyond the moon, He crashed on sheepskin holding wolves within. Goodbye naïve Bo Peep, abandoned soon, In Blackness – roses of ash spread a kin. Along with Jack, they vaulted monstrous walls, But after distant tumbles, cracked a crown. Picking up eggshells, candlestick free-falls Four-and-twenty spiders plummet down. Oh Mary, Mary, does your garden grow? Blinded rats scurry with clocks striking one And crying mercy, broken fiddles throw Another screeching note. Is peace undone? One final comment for/to geese painted blue: Observe the throng that vacates mother’s shoe.
