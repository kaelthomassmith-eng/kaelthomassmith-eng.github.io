Hippie Girl …

*Published in Blood + Honey in 2026.

Hippie Girl;

Or, the Folk Song of Winnie Winters

(TRIANGLE)

There’s ten ‘hers’ for every ‘herself,’

in a T-shirt which reads:

“Art is the way in which you break the rules.”

But give me dialogue without quotation marks.

The eyes are not blind to the violence; they are blank.

(STAR)

Clothes so tight they seem to be holding her together:

where to put ideas if you don’t have a sleeve though?

The subtlety of her words let you grok a feeling,

making them shy children in the modesty of stock.

(SQUATRE)

She’s the heaviest thing in the sky.

(COMPLEX)

“You’re not properly outraged enough,”

So says an angle giving me vibes,

like deep tracks playing into the cerebral cortex.

xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx

Like a flower of infinite petals,

the child of a tie-dyed mind,

so hep in the ways of change --

So is she,

born amongst a canvas of night

wearing her hair like a painting. To…

…Be

‘tween lambs and shepherds.

She lives in the pin of an eye,

feasting on intellectual violence

that no one can deny.

Hippie on, woman; my love,

my life.

Wicked Woman;

Or, there’s nothing else

(TRIANGLE)

In which history’s witchy condemnation reconstitutes itself as a defense --

praise to this is like a dieted ballast,

at once holy and sadistic – like Sade

should he spoke directly.

(STAR)

Her voice is filled with oil,

squatting in a room in my ear,

and I didn’t know if I could ever

leave there.

(SQUATRE)

Wish her mind had a snooze button…

while in these sleeping bags hung on long tree limbs

to bitter winds and worse feelings.

(COMPLEX)

She has

all the side effects and none of the benefits.

xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx

Like a tigress,

chasing us while we ride on

wooden horses on a ground so rough.

Yet she moves with a lightness, an airiness

from bottled storms and like stuff.

A lawyer in pajamas or clothes from the day before,

she’s the Pallas

to every woman before,

pounding drums

with nature’s drunkenness.

Her beauty’s lethal, and her words

acrobatic.
