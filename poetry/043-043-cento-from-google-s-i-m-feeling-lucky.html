Cento from Google’s ‘I’m Feeling Lucky’

*Published in Technoculture in 2018

Despite an inscription in English at the bottom left corner

I began work on this doodle celebrating his work

(many retain their dark pitch-log facades

and take a few deep breaths). 

The place is not to be confused:

dive in to view the world at street level 

even if folded from the same basic plan.

Generosity is contagious:

the whole world, in your hands.

He worked with clay

because of its destiny

to understand history by what he could find in the ground,

exchanging riffs with friends,

hints of the playful transformations prevalent in his later work

He suffered a terrifying health scare,

suffered extensive physical deterioration;

he also needed to express tension: 

“we take death to reach a star”
