Diary XIV

Published in Wire’s Dream in 2018 · Journal
The word ‘doctorate’ just snapped my disorder in two (not that the Other believes in solid duality though). Who the fuck pronounces advertisement ad-vert-is-ment anyway? I ask as every title falls into this category. The smells are as banal as the people, but I know I re-remember them. Yet the canticle of this stupor stretches out the window frame into the house of the psyche – No, I was not roaming free.
