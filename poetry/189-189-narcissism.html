Poem 189

Narcissism

*Published in aois21 in 2013-2018
Is a series of phrases that all fall on the tails side of a coin,
Which are disingenuous to a scrollable font,
Where the first comes last because the first is the last
And the inorganic is brimmed with a growth hormone
To starve a complaint to ill emaciation in a’ sans serif skirt:
This is why the “I” is always capital to none.
