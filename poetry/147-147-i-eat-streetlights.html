I Eat Streetlights…

*Published in Quail Bell in 2018

I eat streetlights in a buffet of night

And look on westward to see th’ rising moon.

I swoon into your generous breath’s tight

Whirlwind, which carves out a magical rune.

Oh, to be th’ sole child of mine own left eye

When desires were but toys I locked away

In an attic of th’ mind. Did you or I

Ever play th’ pan-flute to th’ moon’s gentle sway?

And now I inhale th’ world through a nostril

And have a nightmare in th’ crook of a blink.

Although I'm unseen by th’ eyes of th’ world’s sill,

I know th’ past is too – we’re doomed to sink.

Now, foliage of a thousand eyes in tears

Surround th’ dirty corpse of a teddy bear.
