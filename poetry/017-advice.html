Advice

Published in BlazeVOX in 2018. · Journal
Fetus Words of advice do not mature quickly When spoken into the crook of the ear. They’re dropped on the cold floor --ever sickly, Most aborted in th’ head that fetal year. Child But snuggling to oblivion some go Like orphans placed into a crib of drear. Beholden to laws the homeowners sow, They may grow past the railing like a breer. Pubescent Then, pubescent with a hundred faces, Each cast with different expression strook, Along a hall of mirrors one paces, Its perception like leaves on the wind’s crook: Mature: Chick spanked to ire by the handmaid of time, Only then, that fleeting bird’s caught in lime.
