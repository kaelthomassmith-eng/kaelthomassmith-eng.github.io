Runed

Published in 100 subtexts in 2019-2026 (I think 2020)
The expression was like a rune, Lost into the blank page of history, Which I yet pretended I could Decipher, but it – like these runes Was lost to a second of the past Recorded in ether, but tugging At the heart of the matter, And I raved against The solidness of emotions, Against the names we attachk To every thing we see, gone In the mind after we see it. Boring into the impressionable Inkwell of the past, The expression fleeted, but it Remained an enigma, its Abstraction a gain in form.
