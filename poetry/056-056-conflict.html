Conflict (sonnet)

*Published in Rush in 2026.

When you told me that I was beautiful,

I had no choice but to shrug those words off,

tip my shoulder like I was pouring full

a drink: the toast to which my faults we’d quaff.

When you told me that I was kindhearted

I had to qualify it – pulling out

a godly “possibly,” my lips did part.

Unable to accept, your words I flout.

When you told me that I had such talent

With a’ misleading grin your praise I did halt

for only ‘gainst myself am I gallant

Like lines geographic I laid my fault.

But when you called me depressed to my face

I wondered how you could then join the chace …
