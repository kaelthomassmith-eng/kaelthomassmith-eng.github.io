Poem 49

City Lights

*Published in UnLost in 2018
*Republished in Impspired somewhere around 2018?
Published version ↗
Tomorrow,
The people will face life,
the sober dawn shall take possession,
a different man
has a fever and…
tomorrow the birds shall sing
 
To the people of this city:
our prosperity, like flowers,
the fever will burn up.
For the cure,
We will donate our prosperity:
Rent your attention,
rent your face,
your home
your car.
 
Today,
the people will face
the autumn conquest.
The people will face it sober:
Split your home,
swell your news,
for nothing but what’s returned.
