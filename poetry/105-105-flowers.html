Flowers

*Published in Technoculture in 2018
*Republished in Feed the Holy OR Masticadores USA in 2026

Her tears as black as the ink that describes them,

she broke the word.

we only ever love shadows,

only loved her on the page;

nay -- love on the page, I only loved.

Grecian locks around an army of expressions

held in like a secret;

she winks with both eyes.

i am indebted to

the clouds that surround us

i am indebted to

the animals roaming near me;

she made the sound of a nostalgic

world,

locked a prison holding onto money

with an eye, lips, ear, heart ready to be deflowered again.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter in Chicago
