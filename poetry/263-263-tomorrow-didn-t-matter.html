Tomorrow Didn’t Matter

*Published in Virginia Writers Project 2026

Tomorrow didn’t matter, except it kept arriving.

But it would never come on time.

I thought it rude of it, when I was so dour –

But perhaps that’s only my opinion sour.

Time kept coming like an uninvited guest –

Showing up when I didn’t want it,

And hospitality held me a hostage unfree–

Like a Hamadryad in its tree.

Then yet again, I’m an accoucheur

For yet another dawn’s birth --

My responsibility, growing asunder,

Has ever been my perennial blunder.

For thinking of time moving on is quite specious

Because tomorrow itself never changes,

A rough draft, in the mind ever the same thing,

At once a hope and yet unseen.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
