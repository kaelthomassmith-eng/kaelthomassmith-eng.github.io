Poem 199

Oh, Beautician

*Published in Pilcrow and Dagger in 2018
Oh, lower the face –
to unhook it, that mask
of torturous beauty as solid as powder,
that plastic round curve of your eyne
(old like an archaic noun) and mouth --
so that they are no longer “yours”
(in the Biblical sense). To paint
the lips redder than lips be,
to paint your backstory with a fleck,
like cheeks of that fetishized bean, and see
see what this inner cage be --
from pulling roots like doll weeds
to melting black outlines on
lashes once free.
Oh, self-lover, come back to me
and admire the once and ever gleam
of burnt bliss,
of unreasonable things -- not unlike
the writer writing the self --
of things that simply be.
