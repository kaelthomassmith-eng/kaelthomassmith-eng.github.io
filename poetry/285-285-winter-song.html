Winter Song

*Published in DREICH in 2019

I think our feet are dragging us down,

and we all wish we lived in California,

but I've been blown out by a melted candle

and already your voice is so warm.

So when the cowardly sun hides himself

I'll be young again -- and we'll

go inside to wander, intransitive verb,

get lost in our home’s pen.

But this house has always been shrinking

because winter comes on too fast.

And I only wish that everything were temperate

as you’ve already driven past.
