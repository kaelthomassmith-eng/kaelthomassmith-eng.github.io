Breath (A Herbert-esque wreathed poem)

Published in BlazeVOX in 2018. · Journal
I wanted to inhale the essence of you, but a lewd dream got in the way, strangled my mind’s fays with gestures undue, but perhaps this new horoscope has more sway, for today this Aganippe has overflown into blown hands begging for something more and my breast, perhorresces to loan even one lone thought of you, breathed before.
