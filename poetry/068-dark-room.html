Dark Room

Published in Calliope in 2019
I saw you or the outlines thereof, whose shadows dance to the whims of the light. Wanted to write a poem to if not describe it -- wrap it in a pooling embrace, but the chemicals between us must have been tricky, rolling like the tide in the darkroom of the secrecy of our love, which for you – determined as I was -- to have it born again on the photographic film born again primordially: You only formed slowly In my eyes, me seeing your shadow; your image, always after.
