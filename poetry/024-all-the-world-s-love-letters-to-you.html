All the world’s love letters to you

Published in Adelaide in 2016 · Journal
If bubbling forth, a fountain burst Of ink black with liquidy girth, Geyser of my heart’s form versed, Marching through this fold and firth, Stained watr’y clear this fount of mirth, With silver clouds, of ink droplets, Who reign o’r my Maid of Birth, By soaking these parched outlets, Then paged lines, this stream overflows To populate worlds from e’ry source While in papered towers goes My drenched flood along this course. Quaff a draft of this bright sunshine - Your glance nondistilled freely give – To eye these mirrored words of mine And spin your dear mind pensive On vows writ of many faces, But each and every, one and true: Stone meaning in my spaces, Endless streams of: “I love you!”
