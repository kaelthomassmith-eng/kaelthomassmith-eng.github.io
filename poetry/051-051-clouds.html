Poem 51

Clouds

*Published by Harbinger in 2016
Journal / archive ↗
The hard clouds are nature’s Rorschach test
And all I see are self-sketches of you.
For on pillowy grass -- an outdoor bedchamber --
I’m couched within my homely mind’s confine,
Within battered frames of a thousand films,
In which you define the camera’s starved lens.
I zoom past everything to hollowly see
A thought-bubble in a jump cut
That never left the ground.
The sky is a lonely place for all we know
To float by like time on a sleepy river
(Stop! I want a metaphor as hard as my thoughts --
Sweet like chocolate poured from a silver cup).
