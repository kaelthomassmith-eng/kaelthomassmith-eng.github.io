Poem 45 of 291

Chinchilla

Published in Academy of the Heart and Mind, 24 March 2020
A little guy scurried in the patched grass to climb a mountain as tall as his will. His stomach steered him up the damned pass to heights that greedily starve the air thin so that the heat could not follow his chasse. In the tight grasp of the Year of the Rat, this clumsy refuge bounced himself along. Its effort shan’t make the sojourn done, but will turn “down” to “up” by short furlongs (for pebbles can be planets to some one). But Helter Skelter’s nature’s architect, whose blueprint is reflected in the sky: as the sun hula-hoops round this speck, planting little kisses upon its face so the meek rotate this tight pass’ neck And upwards towards the mountain’s frail point, so this bit fellow has risen to paw: hopping outside of oneself -- “aroint!” This passion is a fundamental law, even to furry, scuttling rodents. Its coat shimmers like a polished garnet -- like the sore moon imitating the sun, even when it falls clumsily down. So it likewise hops, jumps, and falls anon -- an allegory of life, incarnate.
