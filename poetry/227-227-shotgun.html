Shotgun

*Published in Heartland Review in 2019

we knelt like gods on the linen,

whereupon

two mountains our bodies had become,

and rivers were between our lips --

a kiss to taste

the idea of adhesion:

plump those vermilions had become

and ah --that heartbeat

from whence the feeling sprung --

our claws knotted

from that moment on, and our hips

sprung forward like a shotgun

shattering the space between us.

We became tangled and

savagery

never felt so good.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
