Synthetic water; or Plan B

(a satire…I hope)

*Published in Glass Gates Publishing, Kreatif in 2026

Should our fears become manifest –

and that manifestation be absence: namely, our water sipped from the earth as if from a straw of God’s, leaving nothing but the tragic sight of empty pools.

A plan (which is to say plan B) for what we can do afterwards with the best of our abilities: namely, to make and bottle a corporate substitute: Synthetic Water.

“Less effort.”

“More waste.”

Those can be the taglines, which we could also profligate like a chant, as we bottle and market, bottle and market, bottle and market (like a train chugging along) the product -- spread it over the earth, probably not seventy percent of it excepting spreading the name. People will know our product not just through the taste, but through its marketing, which inferior water never had.

You’ll know it’s knock-

off water from that corporate logo,

which will be estimated at a value of

something beyond human life.

Disclaimer: it will be less efficient than real water and probably will not hydrate the body, and even if it did hydrate the body, it might contain heavy metals that we will say are not proven to be dangerous --

But it will be certified and get tax breaks.

It will not quench thirst,

but it will be consumable.

In fact, it will be superior to water in that you will always crave more and therefore will lead to an endless stream of demand – pun intended. In fact, it will be ultra-consumable. The market can be cornered so much that the earth will be square again – downright Pythagorean in all its limits.

For should the planet die,

our bank accounts won’t.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
