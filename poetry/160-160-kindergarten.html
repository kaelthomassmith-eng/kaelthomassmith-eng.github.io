Kindergarten

Published in DREICH in 2019.
Twelve childish smiles sit within a classroom circle,

Whose innocence is known to but an oracle:

One in education will do nothing but fail.

One will be found “guilty” and spend his life in jail

One’s divorce will leave him to live miserably

One’s Chapter 11 takes all she holds dearly

One will need welfare and become a homeless bum

One will erase her whole childhood with Cuban rum

One will lose his daughter and another stepson

One will be told she will never have either one

One will fall ill and spend months alone comatose

One eventually dies from a meth overdose

One will die in a Mississippian hate crime

And one will commit by hanging a suicide
