Emily Dickinson’s Dashes

Published in Vox Poetrica in 2017; Republished in The Lake around this time; Republished in Galway Review in 2026
I mean – oh my – “He aven” God! Have you read a poem -- By Emily Dickinson? It’s like she’s the almighty Typographer. With dashes more important than Punctuation – or word choice. She – Clearly one without an auto-format – Just went nothing but nuts— With the dashes, I mean – not Her -- actual -- mental illness – These lines, these – Are like giggles, filling a page madly – A page that never really ends –
