Unseen Someone

Published in Visions with Voices in 2018
It’s just another night -- out on the patio of the moon With my hands cradling each other and the lilies all in bloom While my shadow’s too shy to stand next to me. In that brief moment, cut from that shrinking world of our own We sold all that we knew, even the ‘what’ that constituted it all. I hid behind my face, knowing you would come too soon. Put a helmet on my opinion, and threw down the stairs like a bowling ball. And on my bedroom walls pin-ups of my favorite philosophy, But only ever in snippets and only ever washed in white. I mourn the years before I learned how to misbehave. It’s not my fault I couldn’t see into the past, And I'm waiting for someone unseen, from out the only book I ever wrote. And I’m waiting for someone unmet, only sketched out in minor rote.
