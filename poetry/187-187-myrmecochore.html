Michael Thomas Smith
Poetry
Poem 187

Myrmecochore

*Published in Danse Macabre in 2018
--fight for you because you can’t
find yourself,
because you weren’t educated to,
and when you get high,
you only feel nothing at all…
 
While prophets roam Manhattan,
the sons of tomorrow happily tiptoe
to greet the coming night
with cloying words
of only a fly’s delight.
 
For those in white robes
may only worship
the tragic end of the day:
a black sanctus
dropped from ears in sync.
 
You only thank the lord
out of fear of some retaliation,
You only put this mask off
to cover it up
with some other--
 
For loneliness is only something
we invent.
You hold on to a torn scribble,
a vestigial of a god,
just as dirt is the remnant of the soul.
 
Biting into the crust of your desire,
you fell asleep in the middle
of a theory. But the ants of the world
spread it around and make it grow --
only because there’s nothing else to do.
