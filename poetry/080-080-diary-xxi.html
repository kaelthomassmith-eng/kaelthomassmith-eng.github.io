Diary XXI

*Published by aois21 in 2016

1. (Lapetus)

I wrote your name on the sky

Then smudged it out with a thumb too-dry.

And so Lapetus birthed with gentle pen

The little monsters of regret inside me,

Who have declared my moral bankruptcy.

2. (Atlas)

And so I raided the wishing-well,

Looking for a lost dime in the Earth's swell.

But drowned for just a two-pence coin

When you weren’t looking at me,

Too busy holding the world upon one knee.

3. (Epimetheus)

And I’m left looking in the margins for a theme

As you put a sundress on a feral dream

(I looked at the receding palm of your hand --

With its long memory line defaced,

Outside my pruned fingers' missed embrace).

4. (Prometheus)

And I said 'and' so many times before

That the breath of life hung on my lips’ door,

So the sunk ship of my mind's foresight

Made me think that other brother rarely spoke.

Lily-livered, even his look back was broke.

5. (Menoetius)

Stretch the world out on its sorry face.

Use your nose to snub every single place

(I think if you listened to me, you’d see how).

So I customized my pain in verse

For this customary departing hearse.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter, scholar, and educator
