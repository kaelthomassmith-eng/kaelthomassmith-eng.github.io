Smiley

Published in Oddball in 2018
The social body has a broken bone and its two-year old gibberish is stuck between my teeth, like how we’re stuck in a moral hollow. Motors work like fatherless cycles (or perhaps it’s vice versa), churning along like a mangy dog drooling up to a bone -- lo and behold! Guess which one? And now we realize the mask we wear is less than nothing but a mass-produced family portrait.
