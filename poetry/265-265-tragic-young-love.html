Poem 265

Tragic Young Love

*Published in aois21 in 2013-2018
Her hips had a certain shy rotation
In a bathtub of utter disrepair
For in the realm of perjury’s nation
Oath’s are downtrodden in a’ blister’s pained snare.
 
Spying a’ man blessed with the throw of a die,
As too-blue tussles hung below her eye
Her sullen gaze had flown by the wayside.
Her sighs, she mangled with sudden deride,
 
And from her lips passed a’ vial o’ roses quick
To shackle young thoughts with manacles thick -
Chains of a sweetness seldom seen or smelled
A lycanthropy o’ Diana’s fire quelled.
 
So to the faucet o’ vows spoke in young rhyme
At least truth can be: they meant it at th’ time.
