History Book:

Or, The Rheology of Emotional Proximity

*Published in Visitant in 2013-2018.

The perforated forms of the people of Lilliput

Cut out of recycled paper

With crayons rub on a face

To self-give a sense of humor with a scribble.

The two-dimensional world of happy folds:

Papyrus hearts of arthritic origami.

Blank people milling around the factory grounds

Fix a porridge bowl of history in pulp

(for the family is a serial number).

Yesterday the screen of life

Could not be washed with a rag -

No wink into an ambiguous nap -

For the caliper is of a singular invisibility.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter in Chicago
