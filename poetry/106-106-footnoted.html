Footnoted

Nobody said
The zeitgeist was ever fair,
But –
At least we can sell the story wholesale.
 
Because all writing must be beyond the writing itself–
That is to say if a thousand monkeys
Typed
For a thousand years, they’d not produce Hamlet
Because they never understood the context
(they’d never even understand they were monkeys).
 
In islands academic, the context is as far away
As Don Quixote’s windmills.
And holding onto an idea we believe in
Is as oleaginous
As holding onto a fish writhing in our hands,
Especially if that fish is symbolic.
