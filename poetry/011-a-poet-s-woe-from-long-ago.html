Poem 11 of 291

A Poet's Woe from Long Ago

Published in Crack the Spine in 2016 · Published/online version · Journal
O Silvia! Why dost that cheek so blush? Lo-Lo in your thighs, a spasm dances, Kissing tracks up your skin, a wave of flesh To shoot arrows in my eyes; our gazes Trip each other, taking her in a sense.
