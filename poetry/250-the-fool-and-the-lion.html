The Fool and the Lion

Published in aois21 in 2013-2018
Pinwheel Of Fortune…on a broken stand. The turn can e’er hit ‘Happy’ “Thank God for poor times,” Says the Fool of a Thousand Grins - For its revolutions Puts a discount on possible words. Do I need to tell you this Is a love poem?
