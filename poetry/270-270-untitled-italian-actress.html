Poem 270

Untitled (Italian Actress)

*Published in Virginia Writers Project in 2026
I want to know an Italian actress of days
gone by,
someone of the stage who acted plays
In a dialect we no longer have
much access to --
Not really.
Of whom the role was written for -- a shining
star
in the twilight of a dying civilization,
who sang her songs wordlessly, but hit all
the high notes,
who embodied the corpse of a once great
Providence,
a divinity coughing in between the set
pieces,
who remained a name
in a book of games and therefore an
enigma
or something for the humanity I know but
you know not,
yet was once played out repeatedly as an answer.
