Poem 63 of 291

Curiosity

Published in Tau in 2014
I heard a noise somewhere in my head And I went looking for it - Into the et cetera of my thoughts. Through blinking windows Shut with a shrug hard I went down a rabbit hole of a perfumed heart. Now, I remember you like the sound Of a deep sea within a conch; Now, I remember you as Layla Remembers Majnun. With a wooden chin, You decided you weren’t going to grow old, And this made perfect sense to me. In the moments of small death, You confronted time in a black alley And spoke on phone lines Crossing the chasm of sorrow. And headless body in hand I stumbled down to Kashmir With my burnt emotions In solid tow. What could you know, but The rest all just faded away; it all Just faded Away
