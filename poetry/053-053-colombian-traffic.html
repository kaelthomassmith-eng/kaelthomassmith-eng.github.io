Colombian Traffic

*Published in Bronze Bird Review, Issue Three, 2026.

Colombian traffic is like a traumatic thought

that never leaves the mind

but clogs the works, by pushing against

other thoughts – bumper

to bumper,

over and

over.

Michael Thomas SmithWriter in Chicago
