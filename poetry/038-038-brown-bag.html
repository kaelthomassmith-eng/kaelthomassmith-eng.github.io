Poem 38

Brown Bag

*Published in Taj Mahal in 2013
Take your breath to the beat of a skiffle song
And wrap your words in a paper bag.
B-Cause cave paintings from broken fingers
Become the symbol of talking hereafter.
The dinner bell knells for the smearing
Of stick figures mating with stick figures.
With a happy round face
And primrose highlights of a rough surface –
It’s an emotional lunch.
Chaos theory produces the whole story –
Every story -
Even if the “kit and caboodle “
Is like a bag too big to hold itself.
But who needs to count to infinity
To know this is life in a brown bag?
