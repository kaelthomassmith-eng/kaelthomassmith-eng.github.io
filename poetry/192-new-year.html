New Year

Published in Southern Poetry Review in 2026 / Republished in Virginia Writers Project 2026 (two different versions of this poem exist in each website, please try to include both)
The new year sneaks up on us Every year, yet before it clasps Its hand over our eyes, we act Like we’ve seen it coming – announcing its presence with loud shouts and popping sirens to warn everyone of the stalker, Who drags us into the new year Whether we like it or not.
