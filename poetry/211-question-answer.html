Poem 211 of 291

Question / Answer

Published in ArLiJo in 2026
I had a sketch in a notebook that I thought answered all the questions of the universe in precis, But now I can’t read the handwriting, which ironically, I’m pretty sure is my own. But had the questions of the universe been definitively answered -- that I do bemoan. I bemoan why the stars are all crowded, at least sixteen to a single constellation: Look how Taurus tries to run away, and how others are shackled in belts. Fleeting, moving, running – so they go along the liens of this skinny papela. All while people are so lonely, led by a question turned soar, Such that Boötes plants the seeds Of the stars for nobody at all. Such that when the spheres turn, They seem to turn away – And so my pathetic sketch has turned away from me, or I from it. Lost within a primer lesson, not taken seriously – an espieglerie. Such is the eternal allure of mystery, which like a reversed medusa Can never be seen again after you look it in the eye.
